## Our Mission

Cosmiclly is a trusted online marketplace connecting people seeking guidance with gifted spiritual advisors from around the world. Whether you're looking for clarity on love, career direction, or personal growth, we make it simple to find and connect with the right advisor — in real time.

## How It Works

### For Clients

1. **Browse Advisors** — Explore our curated directory of verified spiritual advisors. Filter by specialty (tarot, astrology, psychic readings, love, career), price, and availability.
2. **Connect Instantly** — Start a live text chat, audio call, or video call with your chosen advisor. No appointments needed.
3. **Pay Per Minute** — You're only charged for the time you use. No subscriptions, no hidden fees. Add credits to your account or pay directly with your card.
4. **Rate & Review** — After your session, share your experience to help others find the best advisors.

### For Advisors

1. **Apply to Join** — Submit your profile with your bio, specialties, and qualifications for review.
2. **Set Your Rates** — Choose your per-minute rate for chat, audio, and video sessions.
3. **Accept Sessions** — Receive real-time session requests and connect with clients on your own schedule.
4. **Get Paid** — Earnings are deposited directly to your linked bank account via Stripe Connect.

## Our Values

### Trust & Safety
Every advisor on Cosmiclly goes through a review and approval process. We take trust seriously — our platform includes session logging, dispute resolution, and a dedicated support team.

### Authenticity
We believe in genuine connections. Our advisors bring real expertise, empathy, and insight to every session.

### Privacy
Your sessions are private. We use industry-standard encryption, strict access controls, and never sell your personal data to third parties. You can delete your account and data at any time.

### Accessibility
Spiritual guidance should be available to everyone. Our platform works across devices, offers a range of price points, and provides free introductory minutes with many advisors.
