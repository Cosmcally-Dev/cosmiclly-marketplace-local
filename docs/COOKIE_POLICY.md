## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and activity. Cosmiclly also uses similar technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for the Platform to function and cannot be disabled.

| Cookie / Storage | Purpose | Duration |
|------------------|---------|----------|
| Supabase Auth Token | Authenticates your session and keeps you logged in | Until logout or expiry |
| `sb-*-auth-token` | Supabase session management (localStorage) | Session-based |
| Session State | Maintains active session status during calls/chats | Duration of session |

### 2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your preferences.

| Cookie / Storage | Purpose | Duration |
|------------------|---------|----------|
| Theme Preference | Remembers your light/dark mode choice | Persistent |
| UI Preferences | Remembers layout choices and dismissed banners | Persistent |

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies (Future)

We may introduce analytics cookies in the future to understand how users interact with the Platform. These would be optional and subject to your consent. We will update this policy before introducing any analytics cookies.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

### 3.1 Stripe

When you make a payment or add a payment method, Stripe may set cookies to process the transaction securely and prevent fraud. These cookies are governed by [Stripe's Cookie Policy](https://stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal).

### 3.2 LiveKit

Our audio and video call infrastructure (LiveKit) may use cookies or similar technologies for WebRTC signaling and connection management. These are essential for call functionality and are temporary.

## 4. Managing Cookies

### 4.1 Browser Settings

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

### 4.2 Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable essential cookies:
- You will not be able to log in or maintain an authenticated session.
- Live chat, audio calls, and video calls will not function.
- Payment processing may fail.

We recommend keeping essential cookies enabled for the Platform to work correctly.

## 5. Cookie Consent

By using the Platform, you consent to the use of essential cookies as described above. We will seek explicit consent before using any non-essential cookies (such as analytics) in the future.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
