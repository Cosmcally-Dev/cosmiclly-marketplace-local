# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Cosmiclly ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services at cosmiclly.com (the "Platform").

By using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** Full name, email address, username, password, and role (client or advisor) during registration.
- **Profile Information:** Avatar/profile photo, bio, specialties (for advisors), and pricing preferences.
- **Payment Information:** Credit card details and billing information processed securely through Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
- **Session Content:** Text messages sent during chat sessions, which are stored to provide the service.
- **Support Requests:** Any information you provide when contacting our support team.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, session duration, and interaction patterns.
- **Cookies & Local Storage:** Authentication tokens and user preferences stored via browser cookies and localStorage. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Stripe:** Payment confirmation and transaction status.
- **Supabase Auth:** Authentication tokens and session management data.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide the Service:** Facilitate connections between clients and advisors, process sessions, and deliver real-time communication (chat, audio, video).
- **Process Payments:** Handle billing, credit purchases, per-minute session charges, and advisor payouts via Stripe.
- **Improve the Platform:** Analyze usage patterns to enhance features, performance, and user experience.
- **Communicate:** Send service-related notifications, session updates, and important account information.
- **Ensure Safety:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service.
- **Legal Compliance:** Meet legal obligations including tax reporting and dispute resolution.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 With Advisors/Clients

During a session, your advisor can see your display name, avatar, and the messages you send. Advisors cannot access your email, payment details, or other account information.

### 4.2 With Service Providers

- **Stripe:** Processes payments and manages advisor payouts. Subject to [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **Supabase:** Hosts our database and authentication services. Data is stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure with row-level security (RLS) policies.
- **LiveKit:** Provides WebRTC infrastructure for audio and video calls. Audio and video streams are transmitted peer-to-peer when possible and are not recorded or stored by LiveKit or Cosmiclly.

### 4.3 We Do NOT

- Sell your personal data to third parties.
- Share your information with advertisers.
- Provide your data to data brokers.

### 4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Cosmiclly, our users, or the public.

## 5. Data Storage & Security

- **Hosting:** Your data is stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit.
- **Access Controls:** We use row-level security (RLS) policies to ensure users can only access their own data. Advisors can only access sessions assigned to them.
- **Encryption:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
- **Audio/Video:** WebRTC calls use end-to-end encryption. We do not record audio or video sessions.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained for the lifetime of your account. Deleted upon account deletion.
- **Session Records:** Session metadata (duration, cost, status) is retained for up to 2 years for billing and dispute resolution purposes.
- **Chat Messages:** Retained until you delete your account, at which point they are anonymized (content replaced with "[deleted]").
- **Payment Records:** Transaction records are retained as required by applicable tax and financial regulations.

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Access & Correction

You can view and update your personal information at any time through the Settings page on the Platform.

### 7.2 Data Deletion

You can delete your account and associated data through Settings > Delete Account. This will:
- Anonymize your chat messages (replace content with "[deleted]")
- Remove your profile information
- Delete your advisor details and applications (if applicable)

### 7.3 Data Portability

You may request a copy of your personal data by contacting privacy@cosmiclly.com.

### 7.4 Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent for non-essential data processing at any time by adjusting your settings or contacting us.

## 8. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

### For EU/EEA Residents (GDPR)

- **Legal Basis:** We process your data based on contractual necessity (to provide the service), legitimate interest (to improve the Platform), and consent (for optional features).
- **Data Controller:** Cosmiclly is the data controller for information collected through the Platform.
- **Right to Object:** You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Supervisory Authority:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

### For California Residents (CCPA)

- You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
- You have the right to request deletion of your personal information.
- You have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. Note: We do not sell personal information.
- We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we discover that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on the Platform and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
