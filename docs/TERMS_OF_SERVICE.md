## 5. Billing & Payments

### 5.1 Per-Minute Billing

Sessions are billed per minute at the advisor's posted rate. Billing begins when the session status becomes "active" (i.e., the advisor accepts the session request).

### 5.2 Credit System

Clients may purchase credits in advance to fund sessions. Credits are deducted in real time during active sessions. Credit balances are non-refundable except as required by law or at our discretion.

### 5.3 Card Payments (Auth & Capture)

For clients who provide a payment card, Cosmiclly may place a temporary authorization hold for the estimated session cost. Only the actual amount used is captured upon session completion; the remaining hold is released automatically.

### 5.4 Free Minutes

Some advisors offer complimentary free minutes. Free minutes are applied after the client's credit balance is exhausted during a session, serving as a safety net. Free minutes are subject to change and may be limited to one use per client-advisor pair.

### 5.5 Advisor Payouts

Advisors receive payouts via Stripe Connect. Payout schedules and fees are governed by Stripe's terms. Cosmiclly retains a platform commission as outlined in the advisor agreement.

## 6. Session Policies

### 6.1 Session Lifecycle

Sessions follow this lifecycle:
1. **Pending** — Client initiates a session request.
2. **Active** — Advisor accepts; billing and communication begin.
3. **Completed** — Either party ends the session; final billing is calculated.
4. **Cancelled** — Advisor declines or the 60-second ringing timeout expires without a response.

### 6.2 Ringing Timeout

If an advisor does not respond to a session request within 60 seconds, the session is automatically cancelled at no charge to the client.

### 6.3 Session Termination

Either party may end a session at any time. The client is billed only for the active duration of the session.
